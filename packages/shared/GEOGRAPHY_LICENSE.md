# Offline overview maps

Natural Earth 1:50m land and 1:10m populated places, downloaded 2026-09-26.
The more detailed town catalogue includes Colón and other route endpoints
absent from the former 1:50m populated-places subset. Coordinates are city centres,
not a surveyed canal/road track. Existing saved coordinates remain unchanged.
Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
Generated by `apps/desktop/scripts/build-documentary-geography.ts`.
Relief: Natural Earth I with shaded relief 1:50m, v3.2.0, same public-domain terms.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-natural-earth-1/
https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/50m/raster/NE1_50M_SR.zip
Resampled to 5400x2700 WebP by `build-documentary-relief.ts` (932344 bytes).
Coordinates identify cities, not addresses. Maps are geographic illustrations,
not satellite imagery. Land only: no claims about political borders.

## Satellite city views (current default, 2026-09-26)

Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 / EOX: WMS layer `s2cloudless_3857` at
https://tiles.maps.eox.at/map . Its GetCapabilities identifies this specific
layer as CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
https://cloudless.eox.at/pricing explicitly distinguishes free CC BY 2016 from
newer non-commercial layers. Only the 2016 layer is selected; newer layers are
not silently substituted. Attribution is displayed on the animated map:
EOxCloudless https://cloudless.eox.at by EOX IT Services GmbH
(Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016).
Each project stores the bounded JPEG and provenance/license/date/bounds JSON.
Changes: geographic crop, camera scaling, city marker and label. This is archive
imagery, not a current-year photograph. No affiliation/endorsement is implied.

## Legacy detailed street maps (superseded by user request)

City streets, buildings, waterways, parks and labels are OpenStreetMap data,
© OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under ODbL 1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
One bounded Overpass vector extract is cached per coordinate/radius in the
project's `Documentary/Maps`, with original `.osm.json`, rendered `.svg` and
provenance `.json`. A 2400×2400 PNG is cached for fast frame rendering, rather
than repainting thousands of vector paths on every frame. The animation visibly attributes OSM; export credits also
identify the data and license. These files are retained with the project.
No OSM raster tiles are scraped or bulk-downloaded. A failed initial request
leaves the regional overview available and exposes the detail error in the editor.
